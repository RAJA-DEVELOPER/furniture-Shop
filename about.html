Our Journey

Where It All Began

Lumière Home was founded in 2009 by interior designer Elena Beaumont in a small London workshop. What began as a bespoke furniture atelier has grown into an internationally recognised luxury brand with showrooms across three continents.

Our philosophy has always been simple: create furniture that doesn't just fill a room, but transforms it. Every piece we craft tells a story — of the forest it came from, the hands that shaped it, and the life it will become part of.

Today, Lumière Home employs over 200 craftspeople, designers, and interior consultants, united by a shared passion for extraordinary furniture and exceptional living spaces.

Behind the Craft

Where Mastery Meets Artistry

Every Lumière Home piece is shaped by centuries-old techniques, passed down through generations of master artisans who treat each commission as a personal legacy.

200+
Master Artisans

Each piece in our collection begins as a vision and transforms through the hands of our most skilled craftspeople — artisans who have dedicated their lives to the pursuit of perfection.

Hand-Carved Detailing
Every scroll, fluting, and rosette is carved by hand using traditional chisels and gouges — no CNC tooling on finish work
Time-Honoured Finishes
Shellac, French polish, and hand-rubbed oil finishes that develop a rich patina over decades — never mass-sprayed or UV-cured
Joinery Without Compromise
Mortise-and-tenon, dovetail, and finger joints — held by craftsmanship, not a drop of glue or a single screw where it does not belong
Sustainable Sourcing
Every timber is hand-selected from FSC-certified forests, air-dried for a minimum of two years, and milled to maximise yield with minimal waste
01

Timber Selection

Master graders hand-select each board for grain, colour, and structural integrity — rejecting any piece that does not meet our exacting standard.

02

Rough Milling

Boards are milled to dimension, then acclimatised for up to six weeks in our climate-controlled workshop before any joinery begins.

03

Hand Assembly

Every joint is cut, fitted, and tuned by hand. Our artisans take hours to achieve the millimetre-perfect fit that defines Lumière furniture.

04

Finish & Polish

Each piece receives multiple hand-rubbed finish coats, followed by a final French polish that brings the wood's natural depth to life.

Our Journey

Sustainability Milestones

From our founding ideals to our 2030 pledge — every step forward reflects our commitment to a better future.

2009

Founded with Purpose

Lumière Home established with a core mission: prove that fine furniture and environmental responsibility are not mutually exclusive.

2014

FSC Certification

Became one of the first furniture makers in the region to achieve full Forest Stewardship Council certification for all hardwood sourcing.

2018

Solar-Powered Workshops

Installed rooftop solar arrays across our workshop facilities, cutting grid electricity consumption by over 40% in the first year.

2020

Zero-Waste Manufacturing

Achieved zero-waste production — all wood offcuts are repurposed into smaller goods, sawdust is compressed into biomass fuel, and packaging is fully compostable.

2022

Closed-Loop Water System

Launched a closed-loop water treatment system that recycles 96% of process water, making our finishing and staining operations water-neutral.

2024

Carbon-Neutral Shipping

Offset 100% of last-mile delivery emissions through verified carbon credits and transitioned our fleet to hybrid and electric vehicles.

2026

85% Renewable Energy

Now sourcing 85% of total energy from renewable sources — a milestone achieved through continued solar expansion and renewable energy credits.

2030

Net-Zero Operations

Our pledge: achieve net-zero carbon emissions across all operations, source 100% renewable energy, and maintain zero-waste manufacturing at scale.
